MONDAY

🏋️
Strength & Jump Form (30–40 min)
WARM-UP
  • Jumping Jacks × 30
  • High Knees × 20 sec
  • Bodyweight Split Squats × 3 per side
  • Lateral Lunge × 5 per side
  • Leg Swings × 15 per leg

WORKOUT
  • Single-Leg Glute Bridges – 3×10 each side
  • Wall Sits – 3×45 sec
  • Slow Tempo Squats – 3×10 (3 sec down, 1 pause, 1 up)
  • Explosive Squat Jumps – 3×8 (jump high, reset each time)
  • Bodyweight Calf Raises – 3×20 (hold top for 1–2 sec)

WEDNESDAY

⛹️
Plyometrics & Core (30–35 min)
DYNAMIC STRETCHES + MOBILITY DRILLS
  • Leg Swings × 15 per leg
  • Hip Circles x 15 per leg
  • Twist Lunges x 15
  • Arm Circles x 45 sec
WORKOUT
  • Tuck Jumps (jump and tuck knees in) – 3×8
  • Lateral Bounds (side-to-side leaps) – 3×10 each side
  • Broad Jumps (jump forward as far as possible from standing position) – 3×6
  • Reverse Lunge to Knee Drive (start in a lunge and bring knee up into chest) – 3×8 each side
  • Plank to Push-up (start plank, rotate to pushup) – 3×10
  • Lying Leg Raises (on back raise legs to L shape) – 3×15

THURSDAY

🍃
Recovery & Conditioning (30–40 min)
ACTIVE RECOVERY
  • Light walking
WORKOUT
  • Side Plank 30 sec/side
  • Wall Sit × 3×45 sec
  • Bodyweight Calf Raises × 3×25
  • Seated Calf Raise (30 sec each leg)

FRIDAY

💥

WARM-UP
  • Glute Bridge March(in bridge position, raise one knee) x 8 per
  • Knee Hugs to Calf Raise x5 per

  • Hip Circles (x5 forward + x5 backward per leg)

  • Wall Sit × 3×45 sec
  • Glute Bridges – 3×15 (1 sec squeeze at top)
  • Calf Raises – 3×20
